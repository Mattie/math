# Provenance and license scope

The original denominator-extension source and scripts are copyright 2026 Ryan Matthew Casper under the [MIT license](../AffineLogarithms/LICENSE). Original explanatory prose and the accompanying manuscript use [CC BY 4.0](../AffineLogarithms/licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt).

The new modules adapt the arithmetic and endpoint structure in the immutable [AffineLogarithms package](../AffineLogarithms/). They reuse its geometric interpolation, analytic determinant estimates, field construction, approximation conventions and parameter construction. The new mathematical work is the fixed-denominator entry lift, the common selected-minor scalar, its cost bound, and assembly of the nonintegral endpoint and pilot.

The inherited package records its [exact source provenance](../AffineLogarithms/PROVENANCE.md) and [license scopes](../AffineLogarithms/LICENSES.md). OpenAI's `OAI.NumberTheory.PiExponent` library retains Apache 2.0. The rational- and quadratic-logarithm extensions and affine adaptation retain their existing MIT notices. Lean, Mathlib, and the independent checking tools retain their own licenses and are not endorsers of this result.

OpenAI Codex assisted with research, proof development, review, verification orchestration and exposition. Separate model contexts provide independent initial mathematical, specification and manuscript assessments. Model review is not human peer review. No scholarly novelty or optimality claim follows from compilation or kernel acceptance.

Frozen mathematical source is identified by [proof-source-sha256.json](evidence/proof-source-sha256.json). New verification records identify their own artifacts; historical receipts for inherited proofs retain their original scope. Local operational build scripts and raw logs are not part of the intended public distribution.
